## How to Choose Between Them

Start with venue fit. A videographer who has filmed at [San Ysidro Ranch](/blog/wedding-videographer-san-ysidro-ranch), [Ojai Valley Inn](/blog/wedding-videographer-ojai-valley-inn), or Belmond El Encanto already knows the light, the ceremony positions, and the backup plans. Then watch full films, not just highlight reels; ask what's actually delivered and who films your day. Compare on fit, not just price — the cheapest and the most expensive studios on any list can both be wrong for your wedding.
