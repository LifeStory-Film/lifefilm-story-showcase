## How to Choose Between Them

Start with venue fit. A videographer who has filmed at [Pelican Hill](/pelican-hill-wedding-videographer), [Montage Laguna Beach](/blog/wedding-videography-montage-laguna-beach), or [Terranea](/blog/wedding-photographer-terranea-resort) already knows the light, the ceremony positions, and the backup plans — a crew seeing your venue for the first time spends your morning solving problems an experienced team anticipated weeks ago.

Then watch full films, not just highlight reels — a 90-second sizzle can hide a weak edit. Ask what's actually delivered (highlight length, whether you get a documentary edit and full ceremony, and the turnaround), and confirm exactly who films your day. Finally, compare on fit, not just price: the cheapest and the most expensive studios on any list can both be wrong for your wedding.
